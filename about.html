About the Author

Corey L. Johnson

Corey L. Johnson knows exactly how rare his lane is. The numbers on Black male romance authors are thin, and he occupies that space entirely on purpose.

From his home in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, Corey writes contemporary romance and Christian fiction that strips away the fairy-tale filters to look at how real people navigate life and modern love in today's landscape. His stories center Black women and the men pursuing them, trading idealized perfection for sharp, witty, and deeply honest character studies. He is not interested in sanitized drama, just the authentic, messy, and sometimes funny choices that shape a life.

"Stories should move you, and remind you that even in everyday challenges, there's meaning and hope."

His debut novel, The Grass Is Always Greener, dug into family, faith, and second chances in the American South. Since then he's moved into romance, and every story stays anchored by the emotional truth at the center of it.

Whether it's a woman starting over after her wedding falls apart or two people who can't stand each other planning a wedding together, Corey's stories carry the same conviction: people, and the choices they make, matter.
