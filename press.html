About the Author

Short Bio

Corey L. Johnson is an indie author from Murfreesboro, Tennessee, and one of the few Black men writing contemporary romance. He writes stories about Black love that trade fairy-tale filters for sharp, honest character studies of real people. His novel Into You is the first book in the R&B Mixtape Series. He is also the author of the faith-based Rooted in Grace series, beginning with The Grass Is Always Greener.

Long Bio

Corey L. Johnson knows exactly how rare his lane is. The numbers on Black male romance authors are thin, and he occupies that space entirely on purpose.

From his home in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, Corey writes contemporary romance and Christian fiction that strips away the fairy-tale filters to look at how real people navigate life and modern love in today’s landscape. His stories center Black women and the men pursuing them, trading idealized perfection for sharp, witty, and deeply honest character studies. He is not interested in sanitized drama, just the authentic, messy, and sometimes funny choices that shape a life.

His debut novel, The Grass Is Always Greener, dug into family, faith, and second chances in the American South. Since then he’s moved into romance, and every story stays anchored by the emotional truth at the center of it.

Into You, a slow-burn enemies-to-lovers romance, is the first book in his R&B Mixtape Series. Book 2, We Can’t Be Friends, is coming soon. He is also the author of The Seeds We Sow, the second book in the Rooted in Grace series.

Into You

R&B Mixtape Series · Book 1

She plans weddings. He ends marriages. When their worlds collide, the tension is electric.

  • Tropes Slow-burn, enemies-to-lovers, single dad, offices next door, siblings getting married

Into You is a slow-burn, enemies-to-lovers romance about a wedding planner and a divorce attorney whose offices are right next door to each other. Kiana Mercer believes in happily ever after. Darius Steele has made a career out of helping people end theirs. He’s also raising his five-year-old daughter, Amara. They already have enough to argue about. Then their siblings decide to get married, and avoiding each other becomes a lot harder.

Rooted in Grace Series · Book 1

Faith, family, and the choices we make.

  • Genre Faith-based drama, Christian fiction, contemporary African American fiction
  • Published April 2, 2023 · 213 pages
  • ISBN-13 979-8-3898-0379-4 (paperback)

Behind every perfect life lies a storm waiting to break. Mark is a once-proud provider now out of work, desperate to prove his worth. His wife, Terri, is exhausted from the struggle of life and starved for affection, until she’s drawn back to her former college professor, a man who offers the attention she craves, but at a cost that could shatter her family. Quincey is a lawyer turned pastor, shattered by loss and paralyzed by fear, asked to lead others when his own faith is crumbling. As marriages fracture, secrets explode, and faith is tested, these families will learn the hardest truth of all: the grass isn’t greener on the other side, it’s hiding the same dirt.

The Seeds We Sow

Rooted in Grace Series · Book 2

A novel of hard lessons and second chances.

  • Genre Faith-based drama, Christian fiction, contemporary African American fiction
  • Published July 13, 2026 · 353 pages
  • Amazon 4.6 stars (14 ratings), current as of November 16, 2026
  • Formats Available now on Amazon and Kindle+

Five years ago, Julie Perry walked out of her own wedding in front of three hundred church guests and a reality television audience. Humiliated, betrayed, and stripped of the future she thought was hers, Julie fled Nashville for Memphis with one suitcase and no intention of ever looking back. Now she runs New Ground, a thriving nonprofit that helps broken women rebuild their lives. Then Mark Baxter arrives at a leadership conference in Memphis, and the past Julie buried collides with the life she has built. The Seeds We Sow is a gripping story of faith, betrayal, forgiveness, and the dangerous cost of silence.

Suggested Interview Questions

  1. You’re one of the few Black men writing contemporary romance. What pulled you into this lane, and why does it matter to you?
  2. On your site you say, “I just think it’s my mission to restore faith in Black love between Black men and women.” What does that look like on the page?
  3. Into You follows a wedding planner and a divorce attorney whose offices sit next door to each other. Where did Kiana and Darius come from?
  4. Darius is a single father to five-year-old Amara, and you’ve said you enjoyed writing a man who loves being a father but has some things to figure out when it comes to romance. What did you want readers to see in him?
  5. What have readers shared with you about reading Black love written by a man, and has any of that surprised you?
  6. Before Into You, you wrote faith-based drama, including The Grass Is Always Greener and its sequel, The Seeds We Sow. How did that work shape the way you write romance?
  7. Into You is the first book in your R&B Mixtape Series, with We Can’t Be Friends coming soon. How do the books speak to each other, and what can readers expect next?
  8. Your stories are set around Nashville and Murfreesboro, and The Seeds We Sow moves to Memphis. What does writing the South mean to you?
  9. You write both slow-burn romance and faith-and-family drama. What do you want a first-time reader to know about the range of your work?
  10. What kinds of conversations do you hope book clubs, bloggers, and podcast listeners will have after reading Into You or the Rooted in Grace books?
